# Smart Inventory System

## Team Members
-Talal Alshammari

## Abstract
Small and local retail shops often struggle to track inventory accurately, relying on manual records or overly complex enterprise systems. This leads to unexpected stockouts of fast-moving items and overstocking of slow-moving ones. Smart Inventory System is a web application that gives shop owners a simple way to manage their stock: they can add, update, and remove products, track quantities and prices in real time, and receive automatic alerts whenever an item drops below a configurable reorder threshold.
